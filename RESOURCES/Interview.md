# 💼 Interview Preparation Guide

This guide is dedicated to non-coding technical rounds, system design, resume-building (using the STAR method), and behavioral/HR rounds.

---

## 📖 Recommended Books
* **"Cracking the PM Interview"** / **"Cracking the Tech Career"** by Gayle Laakmann McDowell.
* **"System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide"** by Alex Xu - *Crucial for SDE placements.*
* **"Designing Data-Intensive Applications"** by Martin Kleppmann - *Advanced system design.*
