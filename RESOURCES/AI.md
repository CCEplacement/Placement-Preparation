# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Resources

This guide outlines learning pathways for **Python**, **Machine Learning (ML)**, **Deep Learning (DL)**, and **Generative AI**.

---

## 📖 Recommended Books
* **"Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow"** by Aurélien Géron - *Best practical guide for ML.*
* **"Introduction to Probability"** by Joseph K. Blitzstein - *Core mathematical foundations.*
* **"Deep Learning"** by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville - *Deep learning fundamentals.*
